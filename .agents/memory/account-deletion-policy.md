---
name: Account deletion policy
description: Account deletion must be available to Free and Premium users, independently of store billing.
---

Account deletion is available to users on every plan. Deleting a Lumina account removes Lumina-held account data, but does not cancel a Google Play subscription; users must cancel through Google Play to stop renewal.

**Why:** The product requires Free users to have the same account-deletion right as Premium users, and billing cancellation is controlled by Google Play rather than Lumina.

**How to apply:** Do not gate deletion by subscription tier, and do not imply that deleting an account cancels a store subscription. Explain the separate store cancellation step before confirmation.
