---
name: Coach safety policy
description: Human review of possible User Agreement violations across Lumina AI writing features.
---

Potential User Agreement violations in Coach, Ideas, and writing suggestions should be flagged for administrator review rather than automatically blocked by Lumina.

**Why:** The user changed the earlier blocking policy and explicitly selected flagging across all three AI features.

**How to apply:** Continue normal AI generation after recording a flag. Flags are potential concerns, not final violation decisions. Preserve provider safeguards and request validation; do not add automatic account sanctions.

Only the existing Developer Premium allowlisted accounts may review flags.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose that account group as the app's administrators, rather than a separate administrator list.

**How to apply:** Check the server-verified identity against the developer allowlist. Ordinary paid Premium status is not administrator authority, and review access must not depend on a transient billing refresh.

Distinguish assistance that facilitates misuse from legitimate fiction, historical analysis, or critical discussion of difficult subjects.

**Why:** Lumina is a writing tool; topic keywords alone do not establish misuse. The approved scope preserves benign creative writing.

**How to apply:** Evaluate intent and context, including prior turns and document context actually supplied to the AI. Do not expand this into policing all stored writing or changing approved legal copy without authorization.

Retain private writing only for flagged review items and remove the retained request/output text after an administrator records a decision, preserving minimal review metadata.

**Why:** Administrators need enough context to decide, but ordinary writing and already-reviewed text should not be retained in a separate review system unnecessarily.

**How to apply:** Inform the writer when context is retained for review. Keep the review content administrator-only and out of logs or browser persistence.

Classifier and review-storage outages remain temporary failures, not permission to lose flags. Completed flagged replies use normal query credits.

**Why:** The user asked for human review, which cannot happen if a required review record is silently lost; no usable reply on an outage should deplete the allowance.

**How to apply:** Preserve the draft and restore reserved credit when the safety/review service cannot complete. Keep account/date boundaries when restoring usage.
