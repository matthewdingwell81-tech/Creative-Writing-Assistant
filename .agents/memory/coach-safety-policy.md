---
name: Coach safety policy
description: Product boundaries for blocking prohibited Coach use without punitive account actions.
---

Coach should block prohibited assistance and show a brief explanation, without automatically flagging, suspending, or reporting the account.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose blocking with an explanation rather than a review workflow.

**How to apply:** Keep account sanctions and human-review features out of Coach moderation changes unless the user separately requests them.

Distinguish assistance that facilitates misuse from legitimate fiction, historical analysis, or critical discussion of difficult subjects.

**Why:** Lumina is a writing tool; topic keywords alone do not establish misuse. The approved scope preserves benign creative writing.

**How to apply:** Evaluate intent and context, including prior turns and document context actually supplied to Coach. Do not expand this into policing all stored writing or changing approved legal copy without authorization.

Safety-rejected requests and safety-service outages should preserve the draft, not save a completed Coach exchange, and restore any reserved query credit.

**Why:** No usable writing assistance was delivered, and transient safety failures should not deplete the user's allowance.

**How to apply:** Keep safety explanations distinct from ordinary incomplete-generation failures and preserve account/date boundaries when restoring usage.
